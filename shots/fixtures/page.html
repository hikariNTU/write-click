A page to draw over

Nothing here reacts to the gesture. That is the point: the overlay is drawn above whatever the tab happens to be showing, and takes no pointer events of its own.

Draw anywhere

Hold the trigger and move. The stroke follows the cursor in the page's own pixels.

Read the match

The readout names whatever the stroke matches, and says so when nothing does.

Pick a tab

Hold still and the grid opens. Release over a tile to land on that tab.

Where things sit

The tab grid docks to the top edge and the gesture list to the bottom, which leaves the middle of the window to the stroke being drawn. Try D then L for back, or hold still and let the grid open.

Everything the extension draws lives in a closed shadow root, so none of this page's styles reach it and none of its styles reach this page — including the colour scheme above, which changes with every tab in the strip.
